How to Play

1

Tap a vial to pick it up, then tap another vial to pour into it.

2

You can only pour onto an empty vial, or onto the same color on top.

3

Only the matching colors on top move together — and only while there is room.

4

Win by making every vial either empty or filled with one color.

5

Stuck? Use Undo, ask for a Hint, or Restart. No timer pressure — take it easy.

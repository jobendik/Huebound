How to Play

1

Tap a crystal tube to select it, then tap another tube to pour the shards in.

2

You can only pour onto an empty tube, or onto the same crystal type on top.

3

Only matching shards on top move together — and only while there is room.

4

Win by making every tube either empty or filled with one crystal type.

5

Stuck? Use Undo, ask for a Hint, or Restart. No timer pressure — take your time.
